Problem 11 · Site testing

Campus Customs — App Check

Three checks against the running site, each screenshot taken from the live app with the FastAPI backend reading data/campus_customs.db.

1. The assistant checks real inventory

Asked on the Basic Hoodie Big Yale product page: “how many of this do you have left in each size?”

The assistant answers “XS (15), S (5), M (5), L (8), XL (2), XXL (25)”. Two things are being proved at once. The numbers come from a check_stock tool call against the inventory table — they are not invented. And the question never names the product: the shopper says “this”, and the assistant resolves it from the product page they are on.

Verified against the database — SELECT size, quantity FROM inventory WHERE product_id = 'basic-hoodie-big-yale' returns XS 15, S 5, M 5, L 8, XL 2, XXL 25. Every figure on screen matches.

2. A chat question renders product cards on the page

Asked from the home page: “what hoodies do you have?”

The assistant searched the catalogue and the matching products appeared underneath the reply as clickable cards with image, name, category and price. These are the same ProductCard component the Products grid uses, so clicking one opens the normal product page with its size selector and live stock.

Note the count: “We have 27 hoodie designs”, not six. The card list is capped at six for readability, so the search tool returns the true total separately. The raw garment_type column would have found only 5 — it stores 22 different spellings for about six real categories, and the backend normalizes them before counting.

3. Usability feature from Problem 9 — stock visible before the click

Browsing the Quarter-Zips category.

The School Of Engineering ¼ Zip carries a gold “Only XS, L, XL” badge; the two beside it carry none, because they are fully stocked. Before this, availability only appeared on the product detail page — a shopper who wears medium had to open an item, discover their size was gone, and go back. The badge is drawn from the live inventory table and only appears when three or fewer sizes remain, so it always means something.

Qty250025250
In stock: XS, L, XL — exactly what the badge claims.
